# IBM technical authority rules

## Scope

This rule pack applies technical source-to-scene checks to IBM topics: Instana, Turbonomic, webMethods, FlashSystem/SAN, Power/AIX and active-active patterns.

## What it prevents

- Instana cannot present discovery or investigation without the associated coverage, deployment and investigation evidence.
- Turbonomic cannot present recommendations or automation without target, constraint and workflow evidence.
- webMethods cannot present hybrid integration without architecture, interoperability and API-governance evidence.
- IBM storage and Power topics cannot present host attachment, mapping, HA or mobility without their official source linked to the scene that explains it.

## Boundary

Passing the structural source-to-scene checks does not certify a customer deployment. Product entitlement, exact releases, models, firmware, topology, permissions and test evidence remain a human review requirement.
